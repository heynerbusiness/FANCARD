Good to know

How much space and power do you need?

About 8×8 ft and one standard outlet. We bring everything else — booth, backdrop, printer, cases.

Can the cards carry our bar's logo?

Yes — the card back is your canvas: your logo, specials, QR to your menu or Instagram. One firm rule: we never print club, league or brand logos we have no license for. Your guests' photos show what they wear — the design itself stays 100% ours.

How many guests can you handle?

Our line runs at roughly one finished card per minute with two attendants — up to 270 cards in a 3-hour booking.

Do guests pay anything?

No. The bar or host books us — cards are free for guests, which is exactly why the line forms.
